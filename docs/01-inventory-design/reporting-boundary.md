# Reporting boundary

*ISO 14064-1:2018, clause 5.2*

The reporting boundary sets out which emissions and removals are included.
ISO 14064-1:2018 sorts them into six categories (see
[emission categories](../02-quantification/emission-categories.md)).

## Steps

1. **Direct emissions (Category 1).** Quantify these in full. List every
   source: combustion, process, fugitive and land use.
2. **Indirect emissions from imported energy (Category 2).** Quantify these
   in full.
3. **Other indirect emissions (Categories 3–6).** Identify every source,
   then run the [significance assessment](significance-assessment.md).
   Quantify the significant ones.
4. **Exclusions.** Record every source that is excluded, with the reason.
   This goes in the GHG report.

## Gases

Include all seven Kyoto gases where relevant:
CO₂, CH₄, N₂O, HFCs, PFCs, SF₆ and NF₃.
Report each gas separately as well as in total CO₂e.

## Biogenic CO₂

Report biogenic CO₂ emissions and removals **separately** from the
category totals.
