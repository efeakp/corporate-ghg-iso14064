## Recalculation policy

Recalculate the base-year inventory (and every year since) when any of these
changes the base-year total by more than the **significance threshold of
_5_%**, alone or added together:

| Trigger | Example |
|---|---|
| Structural change | Acquisition, divestment, merger, or moving activities in-house or outsourcing them |
| Methodology change | New calculation approach or improved emission factors |
| Error discovery | A significant error, or several errors that are significant together |
| Boundary change | New sources found to be significant |

Do **not** recalculate for organic growth or decline, such as more
production or more staff.
