# The ISO 14064 family

| Standard | Scope | Used here for |
|---|---|---|
| **ISO 14064-1:2018** | Quantifying and reporting GHG emissions and removals at **organisation** level | The corporate inventory |
| ISO 14064-2:2019 | Quantifying GHG reductions from **projects** | Out of scope, unless project-level credits are claimed |
| **ISO 14064-3:2019** | **Validation and verification** of GHG statements | Assurance of the inventory and of forward-looking claims |
| ISO 14065:2020 | Requirements for bodies that validate and verify | Choosing an accredited verifier |
| ISO 14066:2023 | Competence of validation and verification teams | Checking the verifier team |
| ISO/TS 14064-4 | Guidance on applying ISO 14064-1 | Interpretation |

## Verification vs validation

The two words are easy to confuse. In ISO 14064-3 the difference is about
**time**:

| | Verification | Validation |
|---|---|---|
| Looks at | Historical data and events | Future-looking claims and assumptions |
| Typical subject | The annual GHG statement (e.g. "FY2025 emissions were 12,340 tCO₂e") | A reduction target, transition plan or projected reductions |
| Question asked | Is the statement fairly stated and free of material misstatement? | Are the assumptions, methods and limits reasonable and well supported? |
| Covered in | [05-verification](../05-verification/verification-process.md) | [06-validation](../06-validation/validation-process.md) |

## Relationship to the GHG Protocol

ISO 14064-1 and the GHG Protocol Corporate Standard are compatible. ISO
14064-1:2018 groups emissions into six categories rather than three scopes.
See the [category-to-scope mapping](../02-quantification/emission-categories.md#mapping-to-ghg-protocol-scopes).
